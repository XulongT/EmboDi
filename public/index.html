Capture guide and uploads
  1. Take at least four shots from different positions, covering the walls and corners.
  2. Keep roughly one-third to one-half overlap between views. Move around instead of only turning in place.
  3. Keep your head level and include wall–floor boundaries. Add doors, windows, and upper walls when possible.
  4. Hold still while capturing. Focus on room layout rather than small tabletop details.

JPEG or PNG, up to 20 MB per original file. Photos are resized before upload. Every photo is used in batches of up to 6; larger sets take longer.

Builds room structure and large furniture. Each photo is saved locally; the photo list belongs to this page session. Dimensions are estimates and can be adjusted inside the scene.

Calibration and dimensions

Once aligned, move physically to film; stick translation is disabled. Saved offsets are restored against a fresh room scan. During alignment, use the left stick for offset, left grip + stick for height, and A to save. Manual alignment may need to be repeated when room tracking is unavailable.

Dimensions change room proportions, not alignment with the real room. Furniture placement may still need adjustment.

Recording and export

Press B to record before taking photos. Press again to stop.

Exports 1280 × 720 MP4 at 30 fps, processed on this computer. Each take stops and saves at three minutes. Includes virtual hands and arms; excludes the real camera view, audio, menus, and controller models. Files are saved locally for download.
